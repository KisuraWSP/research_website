Research Scope & Gap

Research Problem

Low engagement and retention with static VR exhibits, characterized by limited interactivity and a lack of adaptive guidance.

Objectives

  • Design context-aware VR guides.
  • Implement realistic animal behaviors (herding, predation).
  • Build interactive timelines.
  • Evaluate retention via user studies.

Reference Literature

  • [1] Radianti et al. (2020) - Systematic review of immersive VR in education.
  • [2] Jensen & Konradsen (2018) - Review of HMDs in training.
  • [3] Boer et al. (2022) - AI for adaptive learning in museums.
  • [4] Li et al. (2021) - VR and AI for educational applications.
